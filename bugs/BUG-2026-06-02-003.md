# Bug Record: BUG-2026-06-02-003

## Summary

- Title: Play Playlist action blocks before current video metadata is ready
- Current status: `fixed-pending-verify`
- Severity: `medium`
- Owner: Diane
- Next command: `/sf-test --retest BUG-2026-06-02-003`

## Reproduction

- Preconditions:
  - A signed-in user is on the Play page with a `videoId` route parameter.
  - The video may have just been opened from the queue, or selected through Next/Previous.
- Steps:
  1. Open the advanced Play bottom controls.
  2. Tap `Playlist` immediately after arriving on the video or immediately after Next/Previous.

## Expected Behavior

- The playlist picker opens as soon as the current route has a valid YouTube video id.
- Full cached video metadata may improve the displayed title, but it is not required to add the video to a YouTube playlist.

## Observed Behavior

- The action can block because the Play screen waits for current video metadata even though the add-to-playlist mutation only needs the YouTube video id.

## Evidence

- User report in chat on 2026-06-02.
- Static inspection of `PlayScreen` showed `_showAddCurrentVideoToPlaylistSheet` used the same metadata resolver as the channel-to-feed action.
