# Bug Record: BUG-2026-06-10-001

## Summary

- Title: Feed subscription-channel picker toggles all channels
- Current status: `fix-attempted`
- Severity: `medium`
- Owner: Diane
- Next command: `/sf-ship BUG-2026-06-10-001`

## Reproduction

- Preconditions:
  - Production ReplayGlowz app.
  - Authenticated account with YouTube subscriptions imported or visible in the Feed source picker.
  - At least two subscription channels are listed.
- Steps:
  1. Open a ReplayGlowz Feed.
  2. Click `Ajouter une source`.
  3. Choose `Channels from my subscriptions` / `Chaînes depuis mes abonnements`.
  4. Click one listed channel.
  5. Click the same or another listed channel again to deselect.

## Expected Behavior

- Clicking one channel selects only that channel.
- Deselecting one channel deselects only that channel.
- The user can choose a subset of subscription channels before adding them to the Feed.

## Observed Behavior

- Clicking one channel selects all listed channels.
- Deselecting one channel deselects all listed channels.
- The user cannot select just one channel to add from subscriptions.

## Evidence

- `TEST_LOG.md` entry dated `2026-06-10 - ReplayGlowz Feed Subscription Channel Picker`
- Operator report in chat on 2026-06-10.
- No screenshots or private account data stored.
