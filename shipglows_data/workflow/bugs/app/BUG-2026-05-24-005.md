# Bug Record: BUG-2026-05-24-005

## Summary

- Title: Sign-in page exposes WinFlowz Suite context too early
- Current status: `closed`
- Severity: `medium`
- Owner: Diane
- Next command: `closed`

## Reproduction

- Preconditions:
  - User opens ReplayGlowz while unauthenticated.
- Steps:
  1. Visit `/sign-in`.
  2. Read the sign-in copy and secondary actions.

## Expected Behavior

- The sign-in page should feel like a normal ReplayGlowz account creation/sign-in flow. Parent-company or suite-account context should stay hidden unless the user reaches a support/account-management moment where it is necessary.

## Observed Behavior

- The sign-in page explained WinFlowz Suite and exposed a WinFlowz explainer link, creating unnecessary friction before the user has created or entered a ReplayGlowz account.

## Evidence

- `auth_gate.dart` included `Sign in with your WinFlowz Suite account`, `WinFlowz is the company behind ReplayGlowz`, `What is WinFlowz?`, and `Open WinFlowz account center`.
- `flutter analyze` completed with no issues.
- `node --test api/auth/_youtube.test.js` passed 20 tests.
- Focused `rg` copy check found no `WinFlowz`, `Suite account`, `What is WinFlowz`, `Open WinFlowz`, or `WinFlowz Suite` wording in `app/lib` or `app/api`.
