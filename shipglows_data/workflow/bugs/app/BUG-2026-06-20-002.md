# Bug Record: BUG-2026-06-20-002

## Summary

- Title: Play swipe menu only opens or closes intermittently from the bottom bar
- Current status: `fix-attempted`
- Severity: `medium`
- Owner: Diane
- Next command: `/sf-ship BUG-2026-06-20-002`

## Reproduction

- Preconditions:
  - A signed-in user is on the Play page with a valid video context.
  - The mobile bottom bar is visible.
- Steps:
  1. Swipe up from different parts of the Play bottom bar.
  2. Observe whether the swipe menu appears.
  3. Swipe down on the open swipe menu.

## Expected Behavior

- Swiping up from the Play bottom bar should reliably open the swipe menu.
- Swiping down on the open swipe menu should reliably close it.
- Simple taps should not randomly open or close the swipe menu.

## Observed Behavior

- The swipe menu opens or closes only intermittently, depending on gesture speed and where the swipe started.

## Evidence

- Operator report in chat on 2026-06-20.
- Static inspection showed the gesture path depended on `onVerticalDragEnd` velocity thresholds and overlapping gesture detectors.

## Diagnosis Notes

| Date UTC | Skill | Author | Hypothesis | Findings | Outcome |
|----------|-------|--------|------------|----------|---------|
| 2026-06-20 13:20:00 UTC | sf-fix | Codex | The swipe flow is unreliable because it waits for a fast enough drag-end velocity and lets child gesture detectors compete with the parent swipe detector. | `app_shell.dart` used `onVerticalDragEnd` with a `220` velocity threshold for show/hide, while the Play button also registered its own vertical swipe handler. Normal swipes and mixed hit areas could therefore noop. | supported |
| 2026-06-20 13:55:00 UTC | sf-fix | Codex | The menu buttons feel inconsistent because some actions wait for async data before opening any sheet, and multiple swipe-menu actions can launch concurrently without a shared guard. | `Playlist` waited for `playlistsProvider.future` and `Channel` waited for metadata/feed fetches before opening any modal, so taps could look ignored. The Play screen also had no in-flight gate across hide/watched/playlist/channel requests. | supported |
