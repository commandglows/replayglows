# Bug Record: BUG-2026-06-02-002

## Summary

- Title: Play advanced bottom controls swipe is unreliable
- Current status: `fixed-pending-verify`
- Severity: `medium`
- Owner: Diane
- Next command: `/sf-test --retest BUG-2026-06-02-002`

## Reproduction

- Preconditions:
  - ReplayGlowz is on the Play page.
  - A video context exists through the route `videoId`, the last active Play video, or synced playback state.
- Steps:
  1. Swipe vertically from the Play bottom bar to show advanced controls.
  2. Try the same flow when the natural bottom navigation is visible.
  3. Try the same flow when playback controls are visible.
  4. Swipe down to hide the advanced controls.

## Expected Behavior

- From the Play page with a video context, the advanced controls can be shown reliably with an upward swipe from the bottom bar, even before playback is active.
- The same controls can be hidden reliably with a downward swipe.
- The behavior is deterministic: up shows, down hides, weak swipes do nothing.

## Observed Behavior

- The flow was not reliable.
- The opening swipe was attached to the Play icon inside the natural `NavigationBar`, making the hit area small and susceptible to navigation bar gesture handling.
- The upward swipe toggled state instead of always showing the advanced controls.

## Evidence

- User report in chat on 2026-06-02.
- Static inspection of `AppShell` showed `onVerticalDragEnd` on the nested Play icon in `_buildBottomNavIcon`.
- Static inspection of `_handlePlaybackSeekControlsSwipe` showed upward swipes toggled visibility.
