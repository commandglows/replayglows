# Bug Record: BUG-2026-06-01-002

## Summary

- Title: Feed video snap is not visible
- Current status: `fixed-pending-verify`
- Severity: `medium`
- Owner: Diane
- Next command: `/sf-test --retest BUG-2026-06-01-002`

## Reproduction

- Preconditions:
  - A signed-in user has enough Feed videos to scroll in cards, list, or notes mode.
- Steps:
  1. Open the Feed.
  2. Scroll vertically in any display mode.
  3. Release the scroll while the top viewport is between two video anchors or halfway through a video item.

## Expected Behavior

- Slow scroll release should animate smoothly to the nearest video anchor.
- Fast scroll should remain free and avoid snap interruption.
- The behavior should apply consistently to cards, list, and notes modes.

## Observed Behavior

- Scroll synchronization between display modes works, but video-to-video snapping is not perceptible in any mode.

## Evidence

- `app/lib/screens/videos/videos_screen.dart` used a peak scroll velocity value in the end-of-scroll snap decision, so a short velocity spike could disable snapping even after a slow release.
- The progressive snap window also left a central no-snap zone except at extremely slow speeds.
- Flutter Web wheel/trackpad scroll can fail to produce a useful end-of-scroll trigger, so a snap path that depends only on `ScrollEndNotification` is not reliable enough.
