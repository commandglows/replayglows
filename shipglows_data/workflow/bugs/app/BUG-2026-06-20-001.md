# Bug Record: BUG-2026-06-20-001

## Summary

- Title: Play previous and next controls can noop from the swiped bottom bar
- Current status: `fix-attempted`
- Severity: `medium`
- Owner: Diane
- Next command: `/sf-ship BUG-2026-06-20-001`

## Reproduction

- Preconditions:
  - A signed-in user is on the Play page with a video context.
  - The Play bottom bar is revealed by swiping up from the Play surface.
- Steps:
  1. Reveal the playback bottom bar.
  2. Tap `Previous`.
  3. Tap `Next`.

## Expected Behavior

- The buttons emit playback navigation requests whenever the Play bottom bar is visible in a valid Play context.
- If no previous or next item exists, the UI should fail clearly instead of appearing broken.

## Observed Behavior

- The buttons can appear to do nothing from the swiped Play bottom bar.

## Evidence

- Operator report in chat on 2026-06-20.
- Static inspection of the playback command bridge showed request emission was gated on `hasActiveVideo`.

## Diagnosis Notes
