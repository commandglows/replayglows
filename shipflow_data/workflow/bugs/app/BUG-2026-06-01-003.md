# Bug Record: BUG-2026-06-01-003

## Summary

- Title: Watched toggle refreshes the whole Feed
- Current status: `fixed-pending-verify`
- Severity: `medium`
- Owner: Diane
- Next command: `/sf-test --retest BUG-2026-06-01-003`

## Reproduction

- Preconditions:
  - A signed-in user has Feed videos, including videos marked watched.
- Steps:
  1. Open the Feed.
  2. Press the watched visibility button to hide watched videos.
  3. Observe whether the full Feed reloads or whether watched videos are removed locally.

## Expected Behavior

- Toggling watched visibility should keep the existing Feed data on screen and remove or restore watched videos locally.
- It should not cause the full Feed or selected Feed details to refetch and show a loading skeleton.

## Observed Behavior

- The toggle changes provider arguments, so the app can resubscribe/refetch the video data and show a full loading state.

## Evidence

- `VideosScreen._videosArgs` included `_includeWatched`.
- Selected `virtualFeedDetailsProvider` calls also passed `_includeWatched`.
