# Bug Record: BUG-2026-06-01-004

## Summary

- Title: Feed view modes lose scroll synchronization near the end
- Current status: `fixed-pending-verify`
- Severity: `medium`
- Owner: Diane
- Next command: `/sf-test --retest BUG-2026-06-01-004`

## Reproduction

- Preconditions:
  - A signed-in user has enough Feed videos to scroll in cards, list, and notes modes.
- Steps:
  1. Open the Feed in cards mode.
  2. Scroll near the last videos.
  3. Swipe horizontally to list or notes mode.

## Expected Behavior

- The first video at the top remains the same across cards, list, and notes modes.
- Near the end of the Feed, shorter modes can still scroll far enough to put the same video at the top, leaving blank space below if necessary.
- Non-visible modes stay synchronized while the active mode scrolls, so horizontal swiping feels like moving across one wide synchronized feed.

## Observed Behavior

- Near the end, list and notes modes clamp before the same video can reach the top, so switching from cards can show an earlier video.
- Synchronization is only attempted on view changes, not continuously while scrolling.

## Evidence

- The list and notes `ListView` instances have no large trailing alignment space.
- Scroll mirroring is handled by `_syncScrollPositionBetweenViewModes` during tab changes, but inactive scroll controllers are not kept synchronized during vertical scroll updates.
